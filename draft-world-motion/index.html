MINITUDE: LIVING WORLDS (DRAFT)

The worlds are still pictures today. This draft gives every one a little life while keeping the picture the same: twinkling, drifting, swaying, flowing, glowing. Everything is drawn at the picture's own 320 x 180 pixels with whole-pixel moves, so it stays crisp pixel art. From world 7 on, every world has three designs (A, B, C): pick the one you like. Pick a world, drag the line to compare still (left) with alive (right), and use LIFE to turn the amount up or down (1.0 is the draft; 0 is the original picture).

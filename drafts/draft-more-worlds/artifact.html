Worlds 21 to 30

Twenty candidate worlds, two for each new stretch of size. Every picture moves a little, like the worlds already in the game. Click a picture for the facts behind it. Keep the ones you like and paste the list into the chat.

First, the numbers

World 20 (Collision Hall) covers 380 to 400 points. The 440 you remember was the old Planck Foam threshold, which became 555 on 16 November. From 400, each 20 points shrinks you by a factor of ten.

So reaching 600 needs 10 new worlds (400 + 10 × 20), not 8. With 8 worlds, the unexplored band would start at 560. These twenty designs are two per world, for ten worlds.

A nice fact: with your own formula (size = 1.7 m ÷ 10points ÷ 20), 700 points is 1.7 × 10⁻³⁵ m, the Planck length. A finale at 700 is the physically honest place for it.

The ladder

Each world is 20 points, one factor of ten. After world 30 the unexplored stretch is 100 points (five powers of ten, 1.7 × 10⁻³⁰ m down to the Planck length), then Planck Foam at 700.

What “real” means down here

No experiment has looked below about 10⁻²⁰ m with a collider, so nothing here is a place anyone has seen. Each world is anchored to something that genuinely exists in physics at that energy, and says so in its tag. Energy and length are linked by length ≈ 2 × 10⁻¹⁶ GeV·m ÷ energy.

Achievement icons and the cabinet tab

Draft, 18 Nov 2026. The owner chose the slim bar for the banner and asked for (1) an icon for every achievement and (2) a second tab in the gem cabinet that lists them. Below: the 15 pictograms on their own, four ways to frame them, the cabinet screen with its two tabs (pick a frame at the top), and the slim bar with the new icon. Nothing here is in the game yet.

1 · The 15 pictograms

One drawing per achievement, 24 × 24 pixels, shown here four times larger. The seven rarity firsts are things that get rarer (a pebble, a signpost, a cut gem, a flower, a crown, a lone figure under a star, the rainbow Crescent). The eight gem steps grow: a pouch, a jar, a magnifier, a shield, a pile, a vault door, a half-lit cabinet, a full golden cabinet.

2 · Four frames

The frame takes its colour from the rarity (the gem steps all use the Singularity purple). Earned on the left of each row, not yet earned on the right (grey, a dark silhouette).

3 · The cabinet with two tabs

The same screen as today's gem cabinet, with a tab bar under the title: GEMS (what you have now) and ACHIEVEMENTS (new). Achievements are not secret: every one shows its text, and the gem steps show how far along you are. Earned ones show the day.

On a phone the two columns become one.

4 · The slim bar with the new icon

The banner the owner picked (440 × 58 px, one line of text, one glow pulse), with the framed icon on the left. The frame here follows the choice at the top.

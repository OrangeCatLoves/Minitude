MORE ACHIEVEMENTS · DRAFT

Ideas for the next achievements, for the owner to cut, rename and approve. Nothing here is in the game. There are 15 today; this sheet proposes more in six groups. Every one has its own picture in the same style (24 x 24 pixels, in the tile frame), a name, a one-line text and a note on what it would take to build. A name or a rule can be changed in one line.

All of them would be kept only in the browser, like the others, and would count in solo runs, the Daily and multiplayer unless they say otherwise. The ones judged "when a run ends" show their banner as the report appears. The ones marked "from what is stored" can be given to existing players quietly from their logbook and gem cabinet, with no banner.

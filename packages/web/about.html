About Minitude

Minitude is a free trivia game that runs in your browser. You get a prompt, you type any real answer you can think of, and the rarer your answer is, the smaller you become, from human size down towards the smallest distance that physics can talk about.

The idea

Most trivia games ask a question that has one right answer. Minitude asks a question that has thousands: name a breed of dog, a mountain, a cocktail, a film director, a programming language. Anyone can think of an answer. The fun is in the second one, and the third, and in the answer that only you would think of.

So the game rewards rarity. The answer that everybody gives scores the least, and the one that almost nobody gives scores the most. Every point makes you smaller, and a perfect run takes you somewhere very, very small indeed. It is a game about general knowledge, but also about how your own mind wanders compared with everyone else’s.

What you can play

  • The Daily Challenge: the same seven questions for everyone in the world each day, one attempt, with a streak for the days you finish it.
  • Solo runs: as many runs as you like, whenever you like.
  • Multiplayer rooms: private rooms to play with friends, or public rooms open to anyone.
  • Gems: every question hides one very rare answer. Find it and it goes into your gem cabinet.

There is nothing to download and no account to make. If you would like to know how it all works, read how to play.

The questions

There are hundreds of questions, in categories such as animals, food, games, history and mythology, movies and TV, music, places, science and technology, sports, and transport and vehicles. New ones are added over time. Each question accepts a huge range of answers, from the obvious to the obscure.

If you spot a mistake, or you have an answer that should count and does not, please tell us. If you have an idea for a question, there is a box for it on the page under the report at the end of every run.

The look and the sound

Minitude is drawn in pixel art, and the picture changes with every step down in size: each world you reach is a different scene. The game has its own sound, so it is worth playing with it on. The pixel lettering is Silkscreen by Jason Kottke, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

An independent project

Minitude is an independent project, made by Wei Bin. It is a game made to be played, and it keeps growing: new questions and ideas are added over time. It is free, it does not ask you for personal details, and the privacy page explains in plain words what it keeps and why.

The questions and answers mention many real products, companies, brands, games, films, bands and vehicles. Those names belong to their owners and appear only to say what a question is about. Minitude is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of them.
